1. Data We Collect

When engaging with the Vastraa demonstration platform, data such as cart state, saved wishlist items, and demo simulated orders are stored locally within your browser's private localStorage cache. We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal information.

Customer information provided during consultation inquiries is used exclusively to respond to your specific requests, coordinate custom sizing appointments, and provide order dispatch updates.

3. Cookies & Browser Storage

This website utilizes local storage strictly for functional preferences: preserving your shopping bag between page views, remembering saved wishlist items, and maintaining session navigation.

4. Contact Regarding Privacy

For questions regarding data handling or to request removal of stored consultation records, please contact privacy@vastraa-couture.example.
