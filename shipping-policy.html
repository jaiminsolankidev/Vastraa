Shipping & Delivery Policy

Guidelines and handling timelines for domestic and international ensemble fulfillment.

DRAFT POLICY NOTICE: This document represents demonstration policy terms and requires commercial, logistical, and legal validation before live public launch.

1. Dispatch & Processing Timelines

Ready-to-ship handloom sarees, kurtas, and festive ensembles are carefully inspected, steamed, and packaged in breathable muslin storage boxes within 24 to 48 business hours of order placement.

Made-to-order couture items, custom-embroidered bridal lehengas, and tailored sherwanis require 14 to 28 artisan weaving days as noted on their respective specification pages.

2. Domestic Shipping Rates & Thresholds

We offer complimentary insured express shipping across all serviceable PIN codes in India on orders valuing ₹2,999 or greater. For orders under ₹2,999, a flat shipping charge of ₹199 is calculated at checkout.

3. Transit Insurance & Delivery Partners

All shipments are transported via premium air express logistics partners (BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC). Shipments are fully insured against loss or transit damage until physical handover at your doorstep.

4. Damaged or Tampered Packaging

If your parcel arrives with tampered security tape or visible exterior damage, please refuse delivery and notify our atelier concierge immediately at concierge@vastraa-couture.example.
