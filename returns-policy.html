Returns & Exchanges Policy

Hassle-free 7-day handcrafted exchange window for unworn, unstitched ensembles.

DRAFT POLICY NOTICE: This document represents demonstration policy terms and requires operational review before commercial launch.

1. 7-Day Return & Exchange Window

We stand firmly behind the generational quality of our craftsmanship. If you are not entirely satisfied with your drape, you may initiate a return or exchange request within 7 calendar days of delivery.

2. Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for an exchange or refund:

  • The ensemble must be unworn, unwashed, and unperfumed.
  • All original Silk Mark tags, brand seals, and security ribbons must remain firmly attached.
  • For sarees, the unstitched blouse piece must not have been cut, hemmed, or altered.
  • Items must be returned inside their original protective cedar or muslin gift packaging.

3. Bespoke & Customized Items

Outfits that have undergone custom tailoring, custom fall/pico service, or personalized blouse stitching are bespoke creations and cannot be returned unless found to contain a manufacturing defect.

4. Reverse Courier Pickup

We arrange complimentary reverse courier pickups from your delivery address across most Indian pin codes. Upon passing quality inspection at our Bengaluru atelier, your refund is credited within 5 to 7 business days.
